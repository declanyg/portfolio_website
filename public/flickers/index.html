Flickers

Swipe & Collect — a reflex game for iPhone.

An arrow flashes. Swipe it before it fades. A shape flashes — draw it. Miss three times and the run is over. Every run earns sparks, and sparks buy creatures: fifty of them, each drawn by hand, one so rare most players will never see it.

What's in it

  • Four difficulty tiers that arrive as you survive, not as a menu
  • 50 collectable Flickers across five rarities, plus a hidden Mythic
  • Daily missions that change every day
  • Global Game Center leaderboards, overall and per tier
  • Progress syncs across your devices through iCloud
  • A one-off Remove Ads upgrade, if you would rather not see them
